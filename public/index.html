Multi-Agent Tamed Travel System (MATTS)

LLMs are probabilistic. Corporate budgets are not.

Enterprise AI deployments fail when agents are chained together non-deterministically without strict boundary assumptions. A hallucinating LLM is one unconstrained prompt away from double-debiting corporate cards or leaking sensitive data. MATTS is an architectural proof-of-concept that tames multi-agent networks for high-stakes financial environments by isolating agents around a validated, immutable state bus guarded by pure, deterministic code gates.
